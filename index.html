What each phase is for

Foundation

Days 1–7

Relax, breathe from the belly, find your true low pitch. Don't force depth yet — just build the habit and feel chest vibration.

Build

Days 8–21

Attack nasality hard (ng→ah + pinch test). Extend breath. Ramp articulation. Start slowing down in real conversations.

Integrate

Days 22–35

Carry the practice voice into real life — calls, ordering food, talking to people. Lower, slower, clearer until it feels normal.

Polish

Days 36–45

It's natural now. Focus on presence, storytelling, delivery, consistency. Record a 2-min speech and critique it.

Weekly benchmark read

record the same passage each week

Once a week, read the same paragraph and record it. Compare to last week — your ears are the coach. Tick when done.

Keep in mind

Charisma layer — tone alone isn't enough

  • Slow down ~20%. Rushing pitches you up and sounds nervous.
  • Pause before key words. A pause is power, and it lets your voice settle low.
  • Land sentences down. Ending high (uptalk) kills authority.
  • Vary it. Deep + monotone is boring. Use light and shade.
  • Cut fillers. Silence beats "um / like / matlab".

Health guardrails — read every week

  • Never push into pain, strain, or hoarseness. That's how vocal cords get damaged. Stop and rest.
  • Don't fake a low voice by forcing. Find your relaxed low, don't manufacture it.
  • Hydrate, sleep, don't harshly clear your throat. Voice recovers in sleep.
  • If nasality won't budge after 2–3 weeks, it may be structural (e.g. deviated septum) — see an ENT or speech-language pathologist.
